SETUP

Open the app in Safari. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.

Sign in with Google to sync across devices. Or tap Use Offline.

Go to TOTALS → SETTINGS. Tap the TYPE tile to set your aircraft (A321). Tap ROLE to set FO or CAPT.

For photo scanning: get a free key at aistudio.google.com/apikey. In Settings, tap the GEMINI tile → paste your key → TEST KEY.

LOG A FLIGHT

Photo scan: CAPTURE tab → Camera or Photos → snap the MCDU OOOI page → review → save.

From your schedule: CAPTURE → Schedule Import → upload a roster screenshot or PDF. Each flight appears as a card. After flying, expand the leg → SCAN OOOI PHOTO → pick the MCDU pic → merge panel shows what changed → MERGE SELECTED.

Manual entry: CAPTURE → ENTER LEG MANUALLY → fill in the fields → save.

LOGBOOK

Flights are grouped by month with leg count and total block time. Tap the 🔍 to search by flight number, airport, date, or registration. Swipe left on any leg to delete. Tap a leg to expand and edit any field.

EXPORT & IMPORT

In the TOTALS tab, tap EXPORT for CSV or PDF (CARs 401.08 format). Tap IMPORT to load a CSV or Air Transat PDF logbook.

SYNC & COMPARE

Signed in with Google? Your flights sync automatically. Tap the ⟳ button in the header or in Settings to force a sync.

If two devices show different times, go to SETTINGS → COMPARE. Pick what to compare — times, airports, flight info, or all. The app pulls cloud data, shows each difference side by side, and lets you pick which version to keep. Your choice syncs to all devices.

FAQ

Does it cost anything?

No. App, sync, and AI key are all free.

Is my data private?

Yes. Stored under your Google account. Your API key never leaves your device.

Works offline?

Yes. Syncs when you're back online.

What aircraft?

Any with an MCDU OOOI page — all Airbus, most Boeing.

New phone?

Sign in with the same Google account. Everything loads. Re-enter your Gemini key.

How do I get updates?

Automatic. Or force it: TOTALS → Settings → CHECK FOR UPDATE.
